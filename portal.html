The conversion playbook — scripts, the first-60-seconds framework, and objection handling, ready to use.

Playbooks
The First 60 Seconds ＋
The first 60 seconds set the consult. Greet by name, acknowledge they've thought about this for years, and give permission to act — not urgency. Significance, certainty, connection.
Inbound Call Script ＋
Open warm, qualify gently (timeline, prior experience), book the consult before discussing price. Never quote full-arch pricing on the first call — book the room.
Price & Financing Objections ＋
Price objections usually mask fear, not budget. Reframe to monthly via Proceed / Cherry / CareCredit / in-house, and return to the outcome. Walk the application with them.
Closing Methodology ＋
Full-arch closes days later, not in-chair. Send the leave-behind, the dated next step, and the surgeon follow-up. Tag price-concern cases into the financing nurture.

How the Engagement Score works ＋
This is GoHighLevel's native Engagement Score — the same number on each contact card and in your Hottest Leads smart list. Replies, answered calls, and bookings move it up; silence and opt-outs move it down. Edit the point rules in GHL (Contact → Actions → Engagement Score) and this page follows. Bands: Hot 20+ · Warm 5–19 · Nurture 0–4 · Cold below 0. Credit comes from the intake form (self-reported); last activity is the most recent touch GoHighLevel has seen on the contact, shown in Central time.
