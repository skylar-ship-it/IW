How Partnership Works

A three-layer model, aligned to your results.

The structure is designed so the platform wins only as your full-arch program grows. Exact figures are tailored to your practice and shared in the partner session.

Layer 1

Partnership Retainer

A fixed retainer that funds onboarding, system deployment, and ongoing platform support — paid across the engagement, not as a one-time setup fee. It keeps the program resourced from day one.

Layer 2

Performance-Aligned Fee

A fee tied to your full-arch results. It scales in as volume grows rather than starting flat — so the platform is rewarded as the program produces, keeping incentives pointed the same direction as yours.

Layer 3

Optional Training & Technology Add-Ons

Choose what you need as you scale: cadaver and live-surgical training, digital-workflow training (printing, staining, glazing), photogrammetry and printer integration, and first-case implementation support.
