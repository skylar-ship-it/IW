The Clinical Problem

Full-arch is being marketed by providers who took a weekend course.

Certification Tiers

Where you qualify depends on your training and volume.

Every applicant is evaluated individually. The tier reflects your current standard — and defines the training pathway available to you. Advanced surgeons may already qualify for IW Fellow. Early-career providers and GPs enter as Candidates with a defined pathway forward.

All tiers include a customized plan. Your certification is not a package — it is designed around what your practice needs.

The Process

Four steps to IW Certified.

01
Apply

Submit your credentials, specialty, training history, and annual implant volume. Every application is reviewed individually by the IW clinical team.

02
Evaluate

IW assesses your clinical standard against the Patient Specific Protocol. Gaps are identified — not penalized. Your tier placement is determined here.

03
Train

Complete your tier-appropriate training curriculum — from photogrammetry fundamentals to advanced PSP methodology and AI-guided planning integration.

04
Certify

Receive your IW designation, co-branding assets, and directory listing. Certification renews annually to ensure the standard remains active.
