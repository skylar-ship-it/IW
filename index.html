The Market Problem

Full-arch went corporate — and care got fragmented.

The category is increasingly owned by corporate chains whose model splits a single patient's care across rotating GPs, lab technicians, and non-surgeon sales staff. Throughput is prioritized over outcomes. The profession watched one of the largest players grow fast on technology-and-marketing positioning — then face litigation over clinical quality.

Patients lose continuity. Surgeons lose ownership of the outcome. The opportunity is to build what that model should have been.
